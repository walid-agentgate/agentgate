# AgentGate Outreach — Design Partner Draft

## Target
AI SaaS/startup teams with agents that can execute real side effects: refunds, cancellations, production changes, exports, or CRM mutations.

## Short message
I built AgentGate, a runtime control plane for AI agent actions. It sits between an agent and its tools and can deterministically ALLOW, ASK, or BLOCK sensitive actions, with replayable audit evidence.

I’m looking for 3–5 design partners. We start in sandbox/replay or Shadow Mode, then protect one sensitive tool. The goal is to prove one thing: a blocked action produces zero side effects.

Would you be open to testing one real agent/tool workflow with us?

## Technical follow-up
The integration is designed to start with one tool and one Policy Pack. We provide the pilot checklist, acceptance gates, Attack Lab scenarios, and evidence/replay workflow.

## Follow-up 1
The pilot does not require production enforcement at the start. We can replay representative traffic first, show would-ALLOW/ASK/BLOCK decisions, review mismatches, then enforce one sensitive tool.

## Follow-up 2
If useful, I can run the first test with your refund/export/deployment tool and leave the decision evidence with your team.

## Outreach rules
- Do not claim customer results before a customer exists.
- Do not claim prevention of every prompt injection.
- Do not claim compliance certification.
- Lead with a concrete side-effecting tool, not generic AI security.
