# AgentGate Pricing — Initial Test Model

This is a proposed pricing experiment, not a claim of validated market pricing. Do not publish these numbers as final until at least 3 design-partner conversations test willingness to pay.

## Free / Developer
**$0**

For local evaluation and open-source adoption.

- SDK / runtime
- Policy Packs
- Simulate + Attack Lab
- Local Replay
- Shadow Mode
- Community support

## Pro
**$99/month** starting point

For one production team that needs centralized controls.

- Everything in Developer
- Hosted Control Plane
- Team access
- Central policy management
- Approval workflows
- Replayable audit evidence
- Runtime monitoring
- Usage limits to be finalized from partner data

## Team / Production
**$499/month** starting point

For teams operating multiple agents or tenants.

- Everything in Pro
- Multi-tenant controls
- Advanced policy bundles
- Egress protection
- Security reporting
- Higher usage limits
- Priority support

## Enterprise
**Custom**

For organizations requiring deployment, identity, retention, support, or contractual requirements beyond the standard plans.

### Pricing principles
- Do not charge for a dashboard alone.
- Price around protected runtime usage and control-plane value.
- Keep local evaluation friction low.
- Revisit price after design-partner evidence.
