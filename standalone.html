About MAGON

Ancient roots. Modern control.

MAGON · 𐤌𐤂𐤍 is the umbrella identity behind AgentGate. The name draws from the ancient Punic and Carthaginian name Magon, connecting North African heritage with modern control infrastructure. AgentGate is the first product under MAGON, built to provide runtime control for AI agents.
